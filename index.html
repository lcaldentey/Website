EXPERIENCE

＋2025 – PRESENT

UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCHER

Yip Lab · Columbia

  • I currently work with the Yip Lab at Columbia to investigate techniques in critical mineral extraction and recovery. As our demand for materials like Lithium, Nickel, and Cobalt skyrocket, it is important to look towards alternative and systematic methods of extracting them in a safe and renewable manner. My work currently consists of analyzing the influences of steric variation on ion mobility, and on examining effective Lithium 6-7 separation.

Visit the Yip Lab website ↗

SQM · Chile
